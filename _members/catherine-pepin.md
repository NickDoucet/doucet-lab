Catherine is an M.Sc. student working at the interface of structural biology, protein characterization and biomolecular engineering.  

Her research focuses on the design and characterization of **nanobody complexes** targeting **human RNases**, with the goal of modulating molecular recognition, stability and functional interactions.

Her project combines:

- **Nanobody engineering** and scaffold design  
- **X-ray crystallography** for structural analysis  
- **Protein biophysics** (binding affinity, stability, ligand interactions)  
- **Structure–function analysis** of RNase complexes  

Through this interdisciplinary work, Catherine aims to understand how nanobody design technology can influence biomolecular recognition and to develop next-generation architectures for biochemical and biotechnological applications.
